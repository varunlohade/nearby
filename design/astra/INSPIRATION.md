- Pentagram’s Public Theater identity suggests a tightly set, high-impact headline for the Block Press. [The Public Theater](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-public-theater)
- The Rumsey map collection suggests placing the city name and north arrow at the map’s edges, leaving the interior clear for neighbourhood labels. [David Rumsey Map Collection](https://www.davidrumsey.com/)
