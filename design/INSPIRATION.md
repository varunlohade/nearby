# Inspiration: Circle (map-first neighbourhood chat)

Seven references. Each one gives a single idea to steal. Screenshots are in `ref/` (desktop captures, 2026-11-19).

| # | Reference | URL | Screenshot | What to steal |
|---|-----------|-----|------------|---------------|
| 1 | Apple Maps place card | https://www.apple.com/maps/ | `ref/apple-maps.png` | The map stays the hero. A detented bottom sheet peeks at a place without leaving the map (used in A). |
| 2 | Felt (collaborative maps) | https://felt.com | `ref/felt.png` | Drawn shapes with labels sitting right on the map, like a hand-made poster (used in B's blobs). |
| 3 | Flighty (flight tracker) | https://flighty.com | `ref/flighty.png` | Live status as big quiet numbers on a dark map. No chrome, numbers carry the story (used in C). |
| 4 | Partiful (event invites) | https://partiful.com | URL only (hero uses real photos) | Events with personality: chunky type, sticker energy, "who's going" up front (B's tickets and stickers). |
| 5 | Stamen (map design studio) | https://stamen.com | `ref/stamen.png` | Calm the base map down hard so your own data pops. Every direction recolours the OSM style. |
| 6 | Uber H3 hex grid | https://h3geo.org | `ref/h3.png` | Snap people to a cell, never a point. A hex tile is a privacy unit you can see (A's hex tiles). |
| 7 | Signal (messenger) | https://signal.org | `ref/signal.png` | A plain, trusted chat layout. Each chat screen here keeps that shape so a stock Chat UI kit can be themed into it. |

## How the three directions use them

- **A, Quiet hex tiles**: Apple Maps sheet + H3 tiles + Stamen restraint. White, Geist, one blue.
- **B, Sticker block party**: Felt shapes + Partiful energy. Ink outlines, flat riso colours, hard shadows, Bricolage Grotesque.
- **C, Night activity cells**: Flighty numbers + Voronoi cells. Pure black, amber heat for "busy right now", Instrument Sans, side panel to build.
